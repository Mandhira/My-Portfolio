# StayEasy Guest app 2.5 release notes

**Version:** 2.5.0
**Release date:** 3 November 2026
**Applies to:** StayEasy Guest app for Android 9 and later, and iPhone with iOS 15 and later

> **Sample document.** StayEasy Guest is a fictional product. The features,
> ticket numbers and dates are examples that I created to show a short,
> consistent release-note format.

## New

- **Check in from the app.** Confirm your details, upload your identity document and sign the registration form before you arrive. Open **My trips**, select your booking, then select **Check in**.
- **Save a booking to your calendar.** On the booking page, select **Add to calendar** to create an event with the check-in and check-out times. *(Android and iPhone)*
- **Dark mode on Android.** The app follows your phone's display setting. *(Android only)*

## Improved

- The booking list now loads in about half the time when you have more than 20 trips. (GA-812)
- Receipts can now be shared as a PDF from the booking page. (GA-790)
- The **Contact the hotel** button now shows the hotel's opening hours. (GA-801)

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the app closed when you opened a booking that had no check-out date. (GA-820)
- Fixed an issue where prices over 10,000 rupees were shown without a thousands separator. (GA-806)
- Fixed an issue where the confirmation email link opened the browser and not the app on iPhone. (GA-799)
- Fixed an issue where the search field lost your text after you rotated the screen. (GA-815)

## Known issues

| ID | Issue | Workaround |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GA-831 | The **Add to calendar** button is greyed out on some Android 9 phones. | Update Android to the latest security update, or add the event by hand. |
| GA-834 | A photo of an identity document taken in very low light may be rejected. | Take the photo near a window or a lamp, or upload a PDF. |

## Update the app

The update is free. Open the Play Store or the App Store, search for **StayEasy
Guest**, and select **Update**. If the update does not appear, wait a day, as new
versions reach all users in stages.

## Get help

Contact StayEasy Support at support@stayeasy.example.com and mention version 2.5.0.
