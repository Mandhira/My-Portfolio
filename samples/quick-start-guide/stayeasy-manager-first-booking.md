# Quick start: add your first booking in StayEasy Manager

> **Sample document.** StayEasy Manager is a fictional product. This guide shows
> how I write a one-page quick start.

In about 10 minutes, you will add a room and your first booking.

**Before you begin:** You need your sign-in details and a current version of Edge, Chrome, Firefox or Safari.

## 1. Sign in

Go to your StayEasy Manager address, for example `yourhotel.stayeasy.example.com`. Enter your email address and password, then select **Sign in**. The **Dashboard** opens.

## 2. Add a room

1. Select **Rooms**, then **Add room**.
2. Enter a **Room name** (for example, `Room 101`), choose a **Room type**, and enter the **Price per night**.
3. Select **Save room**.

**Result:** `Room 101` appears in the room list with the status **Available**.

## 3. Add a booking

1. Select **Bookings**, then **New booking**.
2. Enter the guest's **Name** and **Email address**.
3. Choose the **Room**, **Check-in** date and **Check-out** date.
4. Check the **Total price** at the bottom of the form, then select **Save booking**.

**Result:** The message "Booking saved" appears and the guest receives a confirmation email. To see the booking, select **Calendar**.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "These dates are not available" | Another booking uses the room on those dates. Choose other dates or another room. |
| The **Room** list is empty | Add a room first (step 2). |
| "Enter a valid email address" | Check the address for spaces or a missing @ sign. |

**Need help?** Contact support@stayeasy.example.com.
